Featured Cybersecurity Projects

Northstar Financial Services

Phishing Incident Investigation and Threat Analysis

This simulated case study examines a phishing-based credential harvesting attempt involving a fictional financial services organization. The investigation demonstrates email threat analysis, incident response, and application of the MITRE ATT&CK framework.

The case study consists of three interconnected projects:

01 Project - Phishing Email Analysis

Examination of a suspicious email to identify phishing indicators, analyze sender information, assess potential threats, and document indicators of compromise.

Skill Demonstrated: Phishing Detection, Email Analysis, Threat Identification, and Security Triage.

View Phishing Email Analysis

02 Project - Incident Response Report

Investigation of a simulated security alert, including incident classification, event timeline, investigation findings, containment activities, recovery measures and recommendations to prevent similar incidents.

Skills Demonstrated: Incident Response, Security Investigation, Risk Assessment, and Technical Documentation.

View Incident Response Report

03 Project - MITRE ATT&CK Mapping

Application of The MITRE ATT&CK framework to map observed activities from the simulated phishing incident to relevant adversary tactics and techniques. The analysis also examines detection opportunities and recommended security improvements.

Skills Demonstrated: MITRE ATT&CK, Threat Intelligence, Adversary Behaviour Analysis, and Security Documentation.
