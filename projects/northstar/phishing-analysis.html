01 Project - Phishing Email Analysis

Northstar Financial Services

Project Focus:Email Security and Threat Analysis

Incident Date:November 06, 2026

1. Project Overview

This project examines a simulated phisihin email targeting an employee in the Logistics Department of Northstar Financial Services, a fictional financial services institution.

The objective is to analyze the suspicious email, identify potential phishing indicators, assess the assoicated risks, and document findings that support the institution's cybersecurity investigation.

2. Incident Scenario

On November 06, 2026, a Logistics employee, Joaquin Fernandes, received an email claiming that an outstanding invoice required immediate attention.

The message contained a link directing the employee to a fraudulent login page. The employee accessed the link and submitted corporate credentials, prompting a security investigation.

3. Suspicious Email Characteristics

The following information represents the simulated phishing email used in this investigation.

Sender: logistics-notification@northstar-billing.comexample

Recipient: j.fernandes@northstar.comexample

Subject: URGENT: Outstanding Invoice - Immediate Review Required

Date: November 06, 2026 - 9:01 AM EST

4. Phishing Indicators

  • Suspicious Sender: The sender address does not match a verified business contact.
  • Urgent Language: The email pressures the recipient to take immediate action.
  • Unexpectedd Request: The employee was not expecting and invoice notification.
  • Suspicious Link: The embedded link directs the employee to a fraudulent login page.
  • Potential Impersonation: The email attempts to appear as legitimate logistics correspondence.

5. Indicators of Compromise (IoCs)

  • Suspicious sender email address
  • Phishing-domain: northstar-invoice-review.comexample
  • Targeted employee account: j.fernandes@northstar.comexample
  • Suspicious invoice-related email subject

6. Risk Assessment

The email represents a potential credential stealing threat. By directing the employee to a fraudulent login page, he sender attempts to obtain corporate authentication credentials.

The exposure of employee credentials creates a risk of unauthorized access to institutional resources. Further investigation is necessary to determine whether the credentials wer subsequently misused.

7. Recommendations

  • Report the suspicious email to the security team.
  • Block the identified phishing domain.
  • Reset the affected employee's password.
  • Review authentication and sign-in activity.
  • Strengthen employee phishing awareness training.

8. Conclusion

This analysis demonstrates the importance of identifying suspicious email characteristics and recognizing potential phishing attempts

The findings prove the starting poin for the incident reponse investigation documented in Project 02.
