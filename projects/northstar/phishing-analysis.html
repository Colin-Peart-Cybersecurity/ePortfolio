01 Project - Phishing Email Analysis

Northstar Financial Services

Project Focus:Email Security and Threat Analysis

Incident Date:November 06, 2026

1. Project Overview

This project examines a simulated phishing email targeting an employee in the Logistics Department of Northstar Financial Services, a fictional financial services institution.

The objective is to analyze the suspicious email, identify potential phishing indicators, assess the assoicated risks, and document findings that support the institution's cybersecurity investigation.

2. Incident Scenario

On November 06, 2026, a Logistics employee, Joaquin Fernandes, received an email claiming that an outstanding invoice required immediate attention.

The message contained a link directing the employee to a fraudulent login page. The employee accessed the link and submitted corporate credentials, prompting a security investigation.

3. Suspicious Email Characteristics

The following information represents the simulated phishing email used in this investigation.

Sender: logistics-notification@northstar-billing.comexample

Recipient: j.fernandes@northstar.comexample

Subject: URGENT: Outstanding Invoice - Immediate Review Required

Date: November 06, 2026 - 9:01 AM EST

4. Simulation Phishing Email Evidence

The following email represents the simulated phishing message received by the Northstar Logistics Department. It is reproduced for educational analysis and contains no active hyperlinks.

From: logistics-notifications@northstar-billing.comexample

Date: November 06, 2026 - 9:01 AM

Subject: URGENT: Outstanding Invoice - Immediate Review Required

Dear Joaquin Fernandes,

Our records indicate that an outstanding invoice requires your immediate attention.

[SIMULATED LINK; Review Outstanding Invoice]

Please complete this review as soon as possible.

Evidence Note: This email is fictional and was developed specifically for this cybersecurity case study. No actual organization or employee is represented.

Evidence Interpretation

The simulated email contains several charcteristics that warrant further investigation. These include an unverified sender domain, urgent financial language, and a request to verify corporate credentials through an embedded link.

The message is consistent with a credential stealing attempt. However, the email content alone does not establish whether the sender successfully obtained or misused the employee's credentials. Additional authentication records and security logs would be required.

5. Phishing Indicators

  • Suspicious Sender: The sender address does not match a verified business contact.
  • Urgent Language: The email pressures the recipient to take immediate action.
  • Unexpected Request: The employee was not expecting an invoice notification.
  • Suspicious Link: The embedded link directs the employee to a fraudulent login page.
  • Potential Impersonation: The email attempts to appear as legitimate logistics correspondence.

6. Indicators of Compromise (IoCs)

  • Suspicious sender email address
  • Phishing-domain: northstar-invoice-review.comexample
  • Targeted employee account: j.fernandes@northstar.comexample
  • Suspicious invoice-related email subject

7. Email Analysis and Assessments

The suspicious email was examined to identify characteristics commonly ssociated with phishing and credential stealing attempts. The following table summarizes the observations and their potential security implications.

Analyst Assessment

Based upon the simulated evidence, the email demonstrates multiple characteristics consistent with a phishing attempt. The combination of an unverified sender, urgent financial language, and a fraudulent login destination indicates an attempt to obtain employee credentials.

The submission of credential increses the seriousness of the incident. However, credential submission alone does not establish that the account was subsequently accessed by an unauthorized party. Authentication logs and other security records would be required to confirm potential account compromise.

8. Risk Assessment

The email represents a potential credential stealing threat. By directing the employee to a fraudulent login page, the sender attempts to obtain corporate authentication credentials.

The exposure of employee credentials creates a risk of unauthorized access to institutional resources. Further investigation is necessary to determine whether the credentials were subsequently misused.

9. Recommendations

  • Report the suspicious email to the security team.
  • Block the identified phishing domain.
  • Reset the affected employee's password.
  • Review authentication and sign-in activity.
  • Strengthen employee phishing awareness training.

10. Final Findings and Analyst Conclusion

Summary of Findings

The investigation identified a simulted phishing email targeting an employee in the Northstar Financial Services Logistics department. The message used urgent invoice-related language and directed the recipient to a fraudulent login page.

The employee submitted corporate credentials through the simulated fraudulent page. This represents potential credential exposure and requires further investigation.

Key Findings

  • An unverified sender address was identified
  • The email contained language intended to create urgency.
  • The message directed the employee to a fraudulent login page.
  • Corporate credentials wer submitted through the fraudulent page.
  • Further investigation is required to determine whether unauthorized account access occurred.

Recommended Security Actions

  1. Immediately reset the affected employee's corporate password.
  2. Review authentication logs for suspicious login attempts and unusual account activity.
  3. Revoke active sessions and review authentication tokens where appropriate.
  4. Block the identified phisihing domain through appropriate security controls.
  5. Notify the organization's incident response team and document the incident.
  6. Provide additional phishing awareness training to employees.

Outstanding Investigation Requirements

Although the phishing attempt and credential submission have been established within this simulated scenario, unauthorized account access has not yet been confirmed.

The incident response investigation should examine authentication records, account activity, and available security alerts to determine whether the exposed credentials were misused.

Analyst Conclusion

This investigation demonstrates how phishing emails can be used to manipulate employees and expose organizational credentials. Identifying suspicious email characteristics, assessing potential risks, and documenting evidence are important responsibilities within a cybersecurity operations environment.

The findings from this analysis provide the foundation for the 02 Project, which examines the incident response process and the actions taken by the Northstar security team.
