Incident Classification: Suspected Account Compromise

1. Executive Summary

On November 06, 2026, Northstar Financial Services identified suspicious authentication activity associated with an employee account in the Logistics Department.

The incident followed a phishing email by employee Joaquin Fernades earlier that morning. The employee had submitted corporate credentials through a fraudulent login page associated with the phishing campaign.

Northstar's Security Operations Center (SOC) subsequently received a security monitoring alert identifying unusual authentication acitivity assoicated with the affected account.

Tis report documents the investiagation, incident timeline, secuirty findings, containment activities, recovery actions, and recommendation(s).

2. Incident Background

The incident originated with a fraudulent invoice notification email sent to Joaquin Fernaneds at 9:01 AM EST.

The email instructed the employee to review an outstanding invoice through an embedded link. The employee accessed the fraudulent login page and submitted corporate credentials at approximately 9:09 AM EST.

At 9:18 AM EST, Northstar's SIEM generated an alert for suspicious authentication activity associated with the employee account.

The SOC initiated an investigation to determine whether the exposed credentials had been used to gain unauthorized access to organizational resources.

3. Incident Scope

The investigation focused on the following:

  • The affected Logistics Department employee account.
  • Potential unauthorized access to organizational resources.
  • Authentiation activity follwoing the suspected credential exposure.
  • Identification of suspicious account activity and associated security alerts.
  • Containment and recovery of the affected account.

4. Initial Detection and Security Alert

The SOC anayst reviewed the alert and correlated the activity with the previously reported phishing email and credential submission.
